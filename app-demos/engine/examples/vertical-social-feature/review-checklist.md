# Review checklist

- [ ] Watch on a phone-sized viewport at normal speed.
- [ ] Confirm the first frame communicates auto captions without explanatory text outside the video.
- [ ] Confirm the cursor lands on the Generate captions control.
- [ ] Confirm captions remain inside top, bottom, and side social safe zones.
- [ ] Confirm the longest caption line fits without dynamic layout shift.
- [ ] Confirm playback controls and social chrome are not falsely represented as a real platform.
- [ ] Confirm no generative layer changes the product UI or caption text.
- [ ] Record deviations before changing workflow status from `spec-only`.
