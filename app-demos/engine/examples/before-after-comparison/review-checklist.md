- [ ] Confirm before and after screenshots use identical dimensions and content values.
- [ ] Confirm the comparison direction remains consistent throughout the reveal.
- [ ] Confirm labels identify both states without covering table content.
- [ ] Confirm the callout points to the redesigned scan line only after it is visible.

- [ ] Confirm the vertical output preserves a meaningful matched region.
- [ ] Confirm all claims describe UI hierarchy, not unmeasured business impact.
