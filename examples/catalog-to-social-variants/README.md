# Worked Recipe: Ecommerce Catalog to Social Video Variants

This planning case shows how to turn one current product page into controlled social-video variants without changing product truth between platforms.

## Source pack

- timestamped product page
- authorized product images and packaging
- current price or offer, if the ad will mention it
- approved audience, market, and CTA
- prohibited claims and regulated-category notes

## Master 12-second ad

1. **0–2 seconds — Hook:** show the user situation and the product in the same frame.
2. **2–5 seconds — Reveal:** make product shape and scale clear.
3. **5–9 seconds — Proof:** demonstrate one verified feature with a matching close-up.
4. **9–12 seconds — CTA:** show the approved next action without invented urgency.

The master claim ledger, product references, proof shot, and offer are frozen before variants are created.

## Controlled platform variants

| Variant | Change | Keep fixed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok hook test | first two seconds | product, proof, script, offer, CTA |
| Reels visual test | first frame | product, proof, audio, offer, CTA |
| Shorts duration test | 12 seconds to 9 seconds | claim order, product, offer |
| Marketplace version | CTA and safe-zone layout | product truth and proof shot |

Do not change actor, product, crop, script, music, offer, and CTA simultaneously. One-variable tests produce interpretable results.

## Required QC

- recheck the current product page and offer
- confirm every caption and spoken claim
- inspect the product at native video resolution
- verify safe zones and readability for each placement
- fully decode every export and listen to the full audio
- store the file hash and variant variable in the creative test ledger

Platform requirements can change. Reverify them immediately before publication.
