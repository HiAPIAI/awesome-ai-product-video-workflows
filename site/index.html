More than a prompt list

Product truth, visual fidelity, and real QC across the full workflow

  • Every product claim maps to a current source.
  • Product identity anchors persist from source image to every video shot.
  • Task status never replaces artifact decoding, viewing, and audio review.
  • Generation, likeness use, and publication have separate approval gates.
