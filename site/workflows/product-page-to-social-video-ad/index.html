Product Page to Social Video Ad

Convert a current ecommerce product page into a source-grounded hook, storyboard, script, keyframes, video clips, captions, and call to action.

Inputs

  • Current product page
  • Target audience and market
  • Platform placement
  • Approved offer and CTA

Outputs

  • Claim ledger
  • Hook and storyboard
  • Shot prompts
  • Platform-ready rough cut

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Capture current product facts

    Record the page URL and review time. Extract only visible specifications, included items, approved price or offer, delivery region, and brand language. Ecommerce pages change, so preserve the evidence snapshot.

  2. Map one audience problem to one proof

    Choose a real use situation and the product detail that answers it. Avoid generic superlatives. A strong ad demonstrates the product rather than listing every feature from the page.

  3. Build a short narrative spine

    Use hook, product reveal, proof or demonstration, and CTA. Fit the spoken words and screen text to the real duration; remove secondary claims before increasing speaking speed.

  4. Plan evidence-bearing visuals

    Every shot should show the source for the spoken point: a control, construction detail, package item, size relationship, or approved use context. Keep decorative shots secondary.

  5. Assemble and verify the ad

    Match captions to audio, confirm the CTA and offer are still current, inspect product continuity, and create a publication checklist. Do not auto-publish from the generation workflow.

Gates before the next stage

  • Page evidence timestamped
  • Claims mapped to sources
  • Script fits duration
  • Offer rechecked before publication
