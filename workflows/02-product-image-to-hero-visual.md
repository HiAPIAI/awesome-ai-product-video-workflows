# Product Image to Commercial Hero Visual

Transform an approved product image into a reference-preserving ecommerce hero visual or product advertising keyframe before animation.

**Primary search phrase**: `product image to commercial hero visual`

[简体中文](zh/02-product-image-to-hero-visual.md) · [Back to repository](../README.md)

## Inputs

- Approved product source pack
- Product truth sheet
- Creative angle
- Placement dimensions

## Outputs

- Hero-image brief
- Reference-preserving image prompt
- Two or three controlled candidates
- Approved key visual

## Step-by-step workflow

### 1. Choose one visual promise

Define the single thing the image must communicate: material quality, compact size, portability, a control interaction, packaging, or use context. Do not make one frame carry every benefit.

### 2. Lock the product before styling

Place the canonical product reference ahead of mood, lighting, set design, and camera language. Repeat the non-negotiable identity anchors and explicitly ban added controls, altered logos, or duplicate products.

### 3. Design for the next video shot

A beautiful still may be difficult to animate. Give the hero frame clear depth layers, stable contact shadows, plausible hand interaction, available motion space, and an intentional beginning state.

### 4. Generate controlled candidates

Keep product reference, promise, and composition fixed while changing only one variable such as background, lens distance, or lighting. This makes comparison meaningful and avoids random style drift.

### 5. Inspect at native resolution

Compare the original and candidate at full size. Check logos, text, seams, reflections, control count, accessories, fingers, and contact points before approving a key visual for paid video generation.

## Gates before the next stage

- One visual promise
- Product identity preserved
- Animation space available
- Native-resolution approval
