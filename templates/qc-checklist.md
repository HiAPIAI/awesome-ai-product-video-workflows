# Product Video QC Checklist

## Source and truth

- [ ] Original product sources and rights are recorded.
- [ ] Every spoken or visible claim maps to current evidence.
- [ ] Price, offer, availability, and CTA were rechecked.
- [ ] Real-person consent or synthetic-actor disclosure is recorded.

## Product fidelity

- [ ] Silhouette and proportions match the approved reference.
- [ ] Material, color, logo, controls, packaging, and accessories match.
- [ ] Hands, shadows, reflections, and contact points are plausible.
- [ ] Product identity remains stable across every selected shot.

## Media

- [ ] File opens and fully decodes.
- [ ] Dimensions, aspect ratio, duration, and frame rate match the brief.
- [ ] Audio is present, complete, intelligible, and appropriately loud.
- [ ] Captions match the spoken words and remain inside safe zones.
- [ ] No black frames, accidental freezes, watermarks, or broken transitions.

## Creative and publication

- [ ] The first two seconds communicate the intended hook.
- [ ] Each shot has one clear job.
- [ ] CTA is readable, truthful, and placement-appropriate.
- [ ] Required synthetic-media and advertising disclosures are present.
- [ ] Publication was separately authorized.
